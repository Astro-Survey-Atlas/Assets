HOW TO READ A PRODUCT

From sky position to official data

Every product page answers five questions in order, so a coverage map is a usable conclusion rather than a pile of files.

  1. 01Choose a sky areaStart with a survey, release, or point on the sky.
  2. 02See what is coveredThe MOC and preview show where the published layer reaches.
  3. 03Read the precisionICRS, NESTED, order, area, and exact or estimated are stated.
  4. 04Check the evidenceSource snapshots, checks, and hashes explain how the result was made.
  5. 05Go to the archiveThe final link takes you to the official query or data service.
